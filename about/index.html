About this place

Between analysis and imagination.

Hi, I’m Pravan—a computational materials scientist who has always needed somewhere for the less measurable parts of life.

When I’m not building models of materials, I’m usually making something else: a poem, a photograph, a sketch, or a stop-motion world assembled from LEGO bricks. I have written a poetry collection called Words of a Thinkaholic, spent many nights photographing miniatures under a desk lamp, and taught myself Blender to bring small universes to life.

This site is my expressive release: a home for experiments, curiosities, half-finished ideas, and work made for the pleasure of making it. I like stories that blur boundaries—between art and analysis, order and chaos, imagination and truth.

If any of it resonates, stay for a while. If you want the serious scientific side, my professional portfolio is one door away.
